Methodology

How estimated BAC is calculated

BAC Buddy provides estimates to help you understand alcohol consumption, not actual blood alcohol measurements. The app uses a Widmark-style model based on body information, drinks logged, and time elapsed.

  • Personal factors. Estimates use information such as body mass, height, and sex to approximate alcohol distribution in the body.
  • Absorption. Alcohol is modeled as absorbing into the bloodstream at a constant rate over 15 minutes.
  • Elimination. Alcohol is modeled as leaving the bloodstream at a constant rate of 14.8 grams per hour.
  • Logging quality. Estimates only reflect drinks you record. Missed, late, or inaccurate entries change the result.

Important safety information

Informational only — never use estimates to drive

BAC Buddy is intended for informational and educational purposes only. BAC estimates are calculated using a mathematical model and may differ significantly from your actual blood alcohol concentration. Individual alcohol absorption and elimination rates vary, and no mathematical estimate can reliably determine your level of impairment.

  • BAC Buddy is not a breathalyzer or medical device.
  • Never use its estimates to determine whether you are safe to drive, operate machinery, or make medical decisions.
  • If you have consumed alcohol, arrange a sober ride and do not drive.

Support

Help with BAC Buddy

This page is the official support site for BAC Buddy. Start with the questions below. Track your drinks. Understand your consumption. Stay informed.

How do I log a drink?

Open the app and use drink logging to record what you had. You can choose a recent or favorite drink, create a custom drink, and keep timestamped entries that you can edit or delete later.

Why doesn’t the estimate match a breathalyzer?

The in-app value is a mathematical estimate, not a measurement. Food, medication, fatigue, body composition, and logging errors can all cause large differences from a breath or blood test. Never rely on the app to judge impairment.

Does BAC Buddy send estimates to Apple Health?

No. If you allow Apple Health access, BAC Buddy can save recorded alcohol consumption. Estimated BAC values are not exported to Apple Health as measured blood alcohol content.

Where is my drink history stored?

Drink records and profile details used for estimates are stored on your device. Optional Health sync copies permitted drink counts into Apple Health. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I remove ads?

BAC Buddy offers an in-app purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Apple. Restore purchases from the upgrade screen if you reinstall the app.
