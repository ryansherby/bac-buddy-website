Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how BAC Buddy (“the app”) handles information when you use the iPhone app and this support website.

Information stored on your device

BAC Buddy is designed so that your drink log, custom drinks, favorites, and profile details used for estimates (such as height, weight, and sex) stay on your device. The app does not require an account.

Estimated BAC values are calculated on your device from the drinks you record and the personal details you provide. They are estimates, not laboratory or breathalyzer measurements.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional and happens only after you grant permission in iOS. If you connect Health, BAC Buddy may:

  • Read height, body mass, and biological sex to help fill profile details
  • Read and write alcoholic beverage counts for drinks you log

Estimated BAC values are not exported to Apple Health as measured blood alcohol content. You can turn Health permissions off in iOS Settings at any time.

Advertising

The free version of BAC Buddy may show ads through Google’s advertising services. Those services may collect device identifiers and usage information according to Google’s policies. An in-app purchase is available to remove ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. BAC Buddy does not receive your full payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to purchase processing.

This website

This GitHub Pages site is a static support page. It does not use an account system or a custom analytics backend. GitHub may collect standard request logs when you visit the site, as described in GitHub’s privacy documentation.

Children

BAC Buddy is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and the App Store age rating reflects alcohol-related content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, start with the support page. You can also use Report a Problem through your Apple ID for purchase issues.
